# ClinicGuard AI

A privacy-first AI assistant for doctor and therapy clinics that helps clinicians spend less time on administrative work while keeping sensitive patient information under the clinic's control.

## Team

Team members: to be determined.

## Description

ClinicGuard AI is a concept for a secure assistant designed for small medical and therapy practices. It can help clinicians draft visit summaries, organize non-urgent administrative tasks, and find information in clinic-approved resources. The product should prioritize private deployment and strict access controls, with patient data processed only in an explicitly approved environment.

The assistant is intended to support—not replace—clinical judgment. It should not diagnose, recommend treatment autonomously, or make care decisions. Any clinical content must be reviewed and approved by a qualified clinician before use. The project would need privacy, security, and regulatory review before handling real patient information; the initial prototype should use synthetic data only.

## Roadmap

1. Interview clinicians and clinic administrators to identify safe, high-value workflows.
2. Build a prototype using synthetic data, beginning with administrative drafting and approved-resource search.
3. Add authentication, role-based access, encryption, audit logging, and configurable retention controls.
4. Evaluate output quality and privacy risks with clinicians; keep human review mandatory.
5. Consult qualified privacy and regulatory experts before any pilot involving real patient data.
